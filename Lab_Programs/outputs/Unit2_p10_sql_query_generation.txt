Requirement: Name of the student with the highest marks in CSA65 and their department.
SQL: SELECT s.name, s.dept 
FROM students s 
JOIN enrollments e ON s.id = e.student_id 
WHERE e.course = 'CSA65' 
ORDER BY e.marks DESC 
LIMIT 1;
