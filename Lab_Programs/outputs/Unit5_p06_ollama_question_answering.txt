Q: What is Ohm's law?
A: Ohm's Law states that the current flowing through a conductor between two points is directly proportional to the voltage across the two points and inversely proportional to the resistance, expressed as $V = IR$.

Q: What is the difference between stress and strain?
A: Stress is the internal force per unit area that causes deformation within a material, while strain is the resulting measure of that deformation relative to the original dimensions. Essentially, stress is the cause, and strain is the effect.

Q: Give an example where Ohm's law is used.
A: A common example is calculating the current flowing through a light bulb when you know its voltage rating (e.g., 120V) and resistance (e.g., 60Ω), using the formula $I = V/R$ to determine that 2 Amperes will flow.
