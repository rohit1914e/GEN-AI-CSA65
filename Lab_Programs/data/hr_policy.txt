Working Hours
Standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday, with a one-hour lunch break. Employees may use flexible timing between 8:00 AM and 11:00 AM start, provided they complete 8 working hours a day.

Attendance and Late Arrival
Attendance is marked through the biometric system. Arriving more than 15 minutes late three times in a month results in half a day of casual leave being deducted.

Leave Policy - Casual and Sick Leave
Employees get 12 days of casual leave and 10 days of sick leave per calendar year. Sick leave of more than 2 consecutive days requires a medical certificate. Unused casual leave lapses at the end of the year.

Leave Policy - Earned Leave
Employees earn 18 days of earned (privilege) leave per year. Up to 30 days of earned leave can be carried forward, and earned leave must be applied for at least 7 days in advance.

Maternity and Paternity Leave
Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave. Male employees receive 5 working days of paid paternity leave, to be taken within one month of the child's birth.

Holidays
The company observes 12 paid public holidays per year, including Republic Day, Independence Day, Gandhi Jayanti, Pongal, Diwali and Christmas. The holiday calendar is published every December for the next year.

Work From Home
Employees may work from home up to 2 days a week with manager approval. They must be reachable on chat and phone during core hours of 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM.

Health Insurance Benefits
All employees and their spouse and two children are covered by group health insurance of Rs. 5 lakh per year. Parents can be added at a subsidised premium.

Provident Fund and Gratuity
The company contributes 12% of basic salary to the Employees' Provident Fund. Gratuity is paid to employees who complete 5 years of continuous service.

Learning and Travel Benefits
Employees can claim up to Rs. 20,000 per year for approved certifications and courses. Business travel is reimbursed as per grade, with hotel bookings made through the travel desk.

Resignation and Notice Period
The notice period is 60 days for confirmed employees and 15 days during probation. Unused earned leave is encashed in the full and final settlement.
