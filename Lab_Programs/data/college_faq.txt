Q: What is the last date for paying semester fees? A: Semester fees must be paid before the 15th of the first month of each semester. A late fee of Rs. 100 per day applies after that.

Q: Can I pay fees in instalments? A: Yes. Students can pay the tuition fee in two instalments after applying to the accounts office with parent consent.

Q: Which payment methods are accepted for fees? A: Fees can be paid by UPI, net banking, debit or credit card through the student portal, or by demand draft at the accounts office.

Q: Is there a scholarship for merit students? A: Students scoring above 9.0 CGPA receive a 25% tuition fee waiver for the next academic year.

Q: How do I apply for a government scholarship? A: Apply on the National Scholarship Portal and submit the printout with income and community certificates to the scholarship cell.

Q: What is the minimum attendance required? A: A minimum of 75% attendance in each course is required to be eligible for the end-semester examination.

Q: What happens if my attendance is below 75%? A: Students below 75% are not allowed to write the end-semester exam and must re-register for the course.

Q: Is medical leave counted for attendance? A: Medical leave up to 10% can be condoned if a doctor's certificate is submitted within three working days of returning.

Q: When are the CIA tests conducted? A: Continuous Internal Assessment tests are held in the 6th and 12th week of every semester.

Q: How is the internal mark calculated? A: Internal marks are 40% of the total: CIA tests, assignments, lab work and attendance together make up the internal component.

Q: How can I apply for revaluation? A: Apply for revaluation online within 7 days of result publication by paying Rs. 500 per course.

Q: What is the passing mark? A: A student must score at least 50% overall, with a minimum of 45% in the end-semester exam, to pass a course.

Q: When will the semester results be published? A: Results are normally published within 30 days after the last end-semester examination.

Q: Can I get a duplicate hall ticket? A: Yes. A duplicate hall ticket can be downloaded from the exam portal or collected from the exam cell with ID proof.

Q: What are the library timings? A: The central library is open from 8 AM to 8 PM on weekdays and from 9 AM to 4 PM on Saturdays.

Q: How many books can I borrow from the library? A: Undergraduate students can borrow up to 4 books for 14 days.

Q: What is the fine for late return of library books? A: A fine of Rs. 2 per book per day is charged for late return.

Q: Can I access e-journals from home? A: Yes. E-journals such as IEEE and Springer can be accessed remotely using the library proxy login.

Q: Is hostel accommodation available? A: Separate hostels for boys and girls are available on campus with shared and single rooms.

Q: What is the hostel fee? A: The hostel fee is Rs. 95,000 per year for a shared room including food.

Q: What are the hostel in-time rules? A: Hostel students must return by 9 PM. Late entry requires prior permission from the warden.

Q: Is Wi-Fi available on campus? A: Yes. High-speed Wi-Fi is available across the campus and hostels using your student login.

Q: Is there college bus transport? A: College buses run on 40 routes across the city. Transport fee depends on the boarding point.

Q: How do I get a bus pass? A: Register for transport on the student portal and collect the bus pass from the transport office after paying the fee.

Q: Is there a dress code? A: Students must wear formal dress on weekdays and the college uniform on Mondays and lab days.

Q: Are mobile phones allowed in class? A: Mobile phones must be switched off or kept silent during classes, labs and examinations.

Q: What is the anti-ragging policy? A: Ragging is a criminal offence and is strictly prohibited. Complaints can be made to the anti-ragging committee or helpline.

Q: How do I report a grievance? A: Submit a grievance through the online grievance portal; the committee responds within 7 working days.

Q: Does the college provide placement training? A: Yes. The placement cell conducts aptitude, coding and soft-skills training from the second year.

Q: Which companies visit for placements? A: Recruiters include TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Zoho, Accenture, Cognizant and several product start-ups.

Q: What is the eligibility for campus placement? A: Students need a minimum CGPA of 6.5 with no standing arrears to register for placements.

Q: Can I do an internship during the semester? A: Internships are allowed during semester breaks; a two-month industrial internship is mandatory before the final year.

Q: How do I get a bonafide certificate? A: Request the bonafide certificate on the student portal; it is issued by the office within two working days.

Q: How can I get my transcript? A: Apply for transcripts at the exam cell with a fee of Rs. 300 per copy; they are issued within 10 days.

Q: How do I change my elective course? A: Elective changes are allowed only in the first week of the semester with approval from the HOD.

Q: Are there clubs and student activities? A: Yes. There are coding, robotics, music, dance, drama, NSS and NCC clubs that students can join.

Q: Is there a gym or sports facility? A: The campus has a gym, indoor stadium, cricket ground, football field and basketball courts.

Q: What medical facilities are available? A: A 24-hour health centre with a doctor and nurse is available on campus, with ambulance support.

Q: Is there a canteen on campus? A: Yes. The main canteen and food court are open from 7:30 AM to 7 PM.

Q: How do I reset my student portal password? A: Click 'Forgot password' on the student portal and enter your register number to receive a reset link on your email.

Q: Whom should I contact for IT problems? A: Contact the IT helpdesk in the main block or email the IT support team for login and Wi-Fi issues.

Q: Can I get a refund if I discontinue the course? A: Fee refund follows university rules: full refund minus processing fee before classes start, and no refund after one month.

Q: What is the procedure for lateral entry admission? A: Diploma holders can join the second year through lateral entry based on diploma marks and a counselling process.

Q: What documents are needed for admission? A: Mark sheets, transfer certificate, community certificate, Aadhaar card and passport size photographs are required.

Q: Is there a mentor system? A: Every student is assigned a faculty mentor who meets them regularly to track academic progress and well-being.

Q: How many credits are needed to graduate? A: A B.E. student must earn a minimum of 160 credits to be eligible for the degree.

Q: Can I take a course from another department? A: Yes. Open electives offered by other departments can be chosen from the fifth semester.

Q: What is the procedure for a lab record submission? A: Lab records must be completed and signed by the faculty after every experiment and submitted before the model practical exam.

Q: Are online courses accepted for credits? A: NPTEL and SWAYAM courses approved by the department can be transferred as credits for open electives.

Q: What should I do if I lose my ID card? A: Report the loss to the security office and apply for a duplicate ID card at the admin office with a fee of Rs. 200.
