Attendance
Students must maintain at least 75% attendance in every course. Attendance is recorded in each class hour. Students with 65-74% attendance may be condoned on medical grounds with a certificate and a condonation fee of Rs. 1000; below 65% the student must repeat the course.

Leave Procedure
Leave must be applied for in advance through the student portal and approved by the class advisor. Medical leave requires a doctor's certificate submitted within three working days of rejoining.

Examinations
Each course has two Continuous Internal Assessment (CIA) tests and one end-semester examination. Internal assessment carries 40 marks and the end-semester exam 60 marks. Students must carry their hall ticket and ID card to every exam.

Malpractice in Examinations
Possessing notes, mobile phones or smart watches in the exam hall, or copying, is treated as malpractice. Penalties range from cancellation of that exam to cancellation of all exams of the semester.

Passing and Grading
The minimum pass is 50% overall with at least 45% in the end-semester exam. Grades are O (91-100), A+ (81-90), A (71-80), B+ (61-70), B (56-60), C (50-55) and RA (re-appear) below 50.

Academic Regulations - Credits and Progression
A B.E. student must earn 160 credits within a maximum of 7 years. A student with more than 6 standing arrears at the end of the second year cannot register for fifth-semester courses until they are cleared.

Discipline and Code of Conduct
Students must wear their ID card on campus at all times and follow the dress code. Ragging, smoking, alcohol and damage to college property are strictly prohibited and lead to suspension or expulsion.

Laboratory Rules
Students must wear lab coats and closed shoes in laboratories. Food and drinks are not allowed. Equipment must be handled only under faculty supervision, any breakage must be reported immediately, and observation notes must be signed after each experiment.

Computer Lab Usage
Computer labs are for academic work only. Installing software, using pen drives without scanning, or accessing social media and gaming sites is not allowed. Students must log out and shut down the system after use.

Library Rules
Silence must be maintained in the library. Undergraduate students may borrow 4 books for 14 days; a fine of Rs. 2 per day is charged for late return. Reference books cannot be taken out.

Grievance Redressal
Academic or personal grievances can be raised through the online grievance portal or the student counsellor. The grievance committee resolves complaints within 7 working days.
